made the slow way.

No shortcuts, no frozen bases. Just three things we refuse to rush.

N°1

the dough.

Mixed in-house, then cold-fermented for 16 to 24 hours. That's where the flavour, the chew and the light, blistered crust come from.

N°2

the fire.

Two professional ovens running side by side, so fresh twelve-inch pies keep landing on the table the whole time you're serving.

N°3

the station.

Ivory runner, wood boards, gold candle risers. A setup that belongs at a wedding, and that your guests will want to photograph.

good questions.

Where do you cater?

Brampton and all of Peel, plus the wider GTA. A travel fee applies outside Peel.

Is there a minimum?

Our smallest package is 20 live pizzas, usually right for up to 25 guests. Smaller group? Add cannoli and desserts.

How far ahead do I book?

At least 15 days out, since we notify Peel Public Health for every event. Weekends go first. A 30% deposit holds your date.
